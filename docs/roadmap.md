# Roadmap

Sagip will remain testnet-only until custody, regulation, security, and operational requirements for real funds are completed.

## Current priorities

- Keep the sender, primary receiver, and backup beneficiary journeys simple and understandable.
- Expand settlement, retry, and reconciliation testing.
- Improve reminders and claim-link recovery reliability.
- Strengthen privacy, rate limiting, and abuse protection.
- Add clearer administrator operational alerts.
- Validate the product with OFW families and non-technical recipients.

## Production discovery

- Identify licensed Philippine fiat and asset partners.
- Define the production custody model.
- Complete legal and regulatory assessment.
- Design KMS or HSM signing, multisig, key rotation, and recovery.
- Define reconciliation, incident, dispute, and customer-support processes.
- Complete an independent security review.

## Product improvements

- Accessibility and low-connectivity testing.
- Safer recovery for lost links and devices.
- Clearer consent and education around check-in lapse.
- Better transaction and reminder history.
- Partner-ready operational reporting.

## Out of scope until separately approved

- Mainnet launch.
- Real peso custody or transfer.
- Insurance claims.
- Legal death verification.
- Estate administration.
- Lending, yield promises, or investment returns.
