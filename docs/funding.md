# Funding and Partner Readiness

This document tracks improvements that strengthen Sagip for judges, funders, and potential partners without changing the core family safety-net journey.

## Implemented foundations

- Configurable Stellar testnet held asset with XLM fallback.
- Real claimable-balance transactions and explorer-linked receipts.
- Email reminders for safety nets approaching the end of a check-in window.
- Dashboard reminders and clear trust messaging.
- Separate administrator authentication and transaction visibility.
- Wallet onboarding, recovery-key guidance, and multi-wallet management.
- End-to-end testnet settlement testing.

## Evidence to maintain

- Public testnet transaction links.
- Repeatable demo setup instructions.
- Health and settlement validation results.
- Clear separation between implemented behavior and simulated fiat movement.
- Current trust boundaries and custody risks.
- Screenshots or recordings of the core sender and receiver journey.

## Partner requirements

A production launch involving real Philippine peso deposits or withdrawals requires regulated partners for:

- fiat onboarding and cash-out;
- customer verification and compliance;
- asset issuance or settlement;
- custody and key-management controls;
- transaction monitoring and reconciliation;
- customer support and incident response.

## Funding priorities

1. Licensed anchor or custodial partner integration.
2. Independent security review.
3. KMS or HSM-backed signer operations.
4. Production reconciliation and monitoring.
5. Reminder delivery reliability.
6. Customer research with OFW families.
7. Legal and regulatory assessment.
8. Mainnet operations and recovery planning.

Sagip must remain clearly labeled as testnet software until those requirements are complete.
